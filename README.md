### What I've Built

#### [Trader POS](https://traderpos.app)

Point of Sale and inventory platform built for small businesses in Ghana.

Designed, engineered, deployed, and shipped the product end-to-end across iOS and Android, covering the mobile application, backend APIs, database architecture, offline synchronization, production infrastructure, and release process.

**Highlights**
- Fast POS checkout with multiple payment methods
- Offline-first sales with automatic cloud synchronization
- Product, inventory, and stock movement management
- Barcode scanning and product lookup
- Customer credit and debt tracking
- Bluetooth thermal receipt printing
- Digital and PDF receipts
- Sales, revenue, and profit reporting
- Multi-user store access and staff roles
- Production deployment across development, staging, and production environments

**Stack:** Flutter · Dart · BLoC · Drift · Node.js · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · Prisma · Firebase · Docker · Nginx

🌐 [traderpos.app](https://traderpos.app)
